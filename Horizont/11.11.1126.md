Sea of Franset at 11:11. No overcast. Fairly Windy.  
  
Nelu had successfully stolen a merchant ship and is now exploring its contents. She is confident that the ship is empty. She counted the heads of the crew members before this. Her intuition is correct. There are no other people on the ship except her.  
  
“Hyoo! That just leaves the cabin.”  
  
Nelu wiggled the door open. It is locked and requires a key, Nelu does not have the key.  
  
“Looks like I’ll have to use force on this one.”  
  
Nelu fired her pistol on the lock, successfully opening the door. However, she found that the door is cut too tall as it is scraping against the floorboards. She would need to use her own anatomy to ram the door open. Although Nelu looks slim in frame, her body is able to output the necessary force to open the door.  
  
And so she did. Out of breath and visibly panting.  
  
“Hah… Hellooo! Anyone in heeere?”  
  
There is nobody in the room. It contained a wardrobe, a wine rack, bookshelves, a bed built by the hull, a drawer underneath the bed, a chair and a table. Nelu inspected the bed first.  
  
“Nice! Thought it’d be smaller, very happy with this. I’m not touching it, though. Who knows how many bed bugs there are in it. I’ll clean you up later.”  
  
Nelu then checked the drawer to inspect its contents. She found silver and jewelry, but later found a compartment hidden behind a board.  
  
“Oh? What’s this?”  
  
Nelu found a folded piece of vellum which she initially assumed to be a map, she later finds that she is correct. Nelu does not recognize where it points to, however.  
  
“That’s definitely not Yurova. I know that.”  
  
Indeed it is not. It is a map of Alkeluna, which Nelu did not recognize at the time. Writing on the back of them map shows coordinates and instructions.  
  
“A treasure of sorts? Exciting. I’m assuming there’s treasure, but I’ll have to know what this map maps out of.”  
  
Nelu dropped on the idea for now and focused on settling in on her ship. Nelu proceeded with drying the bed first, and so she did, laying it on the railings with its bedsheets off. Nelu picked up a ramrod, a tool used for loading cannonballs into cannons, and uses to whack the bed.  
  
“Ei, ei, ei!”  
  
After 7 minutes and 12 seconds, Nelu leaves it to dry. Nelu proceeds with picking up a feather duster from the cabin to dust off her, soon to be, room of stay. Starting off with the bookshelves.  
  
“Navigation, manuals ,medicine, maritime Laws, yada-yada…”  
  
Nelu found no book on religion, but she did find a book containing instructions for identifying and avoiding sodomites, as well as legal procedures governing the use of torture to extract confessions from them during trials.[^1] Nelu covered her mouth with her left hand while her other hand held the book. Nelu closed the book and took it outside to throw it into the sea. Nelu‘s face is visibly disgusted by that book.  
  
“I feel dirty now…”  
  
After 6 minutes and 33 seconds, Nelu was able to get a barrel of grog onto the deck, only to dump it into the sea. Nelu intends to use the barrel to hold clean water in order to wash herself.  
After cleaning the inside of the barrel for 8 minutes, Nelu put her right palm over the barrel and used hylogenesis[^2] to fill the barrel with water. Nelu then took off her garments, her undergarments, and her hair tie which held up her hair bun. Before submerging herself inside the barrel, Nelu used manokinesis[^3] to heat up the water inside the barrel to a comfortable temperature. Then, she finally puts herself feet first into the warm water.  
  
”Haaa… That’s nice.“  
  
Nelu comments on how much of she exhausted her mana[^4] and how that didn’t matter because of the relief that she gave to herself.  
  
After 24 minutes and 50 seconds of washing herself, Nelu is reminded that the towel is inside the cabin.  
  
“No one’s looking, right? I can— Who am kidding? I just bathed in the open!”  
  
Gathering the courage, Nelu lifted herself off the barrel and walked towards the broken door of the cabin. Inside she was able to dry herself, Nelu did not realize that there’s only one towel in the ship. It is presumed that the towel belonged to the captain, but seeing how clean it was, Nelu assumed that it was not used at all.  
  
“Was this? Nah, I’ve gone too far. No going back now.”  
  
With no other towel, instead of covering herself from no one (except me), she wraps the towel on her hair to dry it out. Then, she went to do another chore, laundry.  
  
It is 12:08 noon.  
  
Nelu is leaving her clothes to dry in the sun. Her hair is still damp so she decides to keep the towel on her head for now.  
  
“This is stupid. I’ve found more maps than towels. This is actually stupid. Well, if it makes me feel better I know where to stop for food. This vacant island will do, I’m hungry for some crabs.”  
  
This is Nelu’s second time trying to operate a ship after the heist she did in order to escape the owners and the law enforcers. Though, she did not do much in operating as she had already cut their sails to limit their chase. Despite Nelu’s lack of experience, she was quick to steer her ship  towards her desired direction.  
  
After 34 minutes of sailing, Nelu had arrived at her destination.  
  
“Looks like company.”  
  
What Nelu saw is another ship.  
  
“Better announce myself. Don’t wanna give any kind of misunderstandings.”  
  
Nelu stepped back from the helm and avoided a lead ball aimed for her.  
  
“Huh. Maybe they just— misfired?”  
  
Nelu stepped back again and avoided another shot.  
  
“Okay, these folks don’t seem that friendly.”  
  
Nelu flashed her pistol and aimed it at the shooter. She fired her weapon and the shooter is down laying on the sand. The people with guns shot at Nelu while the rest of them retreated into the foliage. They are unable to hit her from such a distance, they need to aim up from the beach and to the ship Nelu is sniping from with her pistol.  
  
“Two, three… four, five… six, and seven.”  
  
Nelu counted seven shots which aligned with the bodies on the beach; then, she loaded another magazine in her pistol.  
  
“The rest of them probably don’t have guns. I’ll just use my sword then.”  
  
Nelu docked her ship near the shore and went down to the shallows by rope. She proceeds to search the seven bodies. All of which have muskets on their person. One had whiskey which Nelu took for herself. Among them were Pierre Dubois, Jean Lefèvre, Laurent Caron, and Marcel Bélin.  
  
“It’s just me and how many is in that ship and how many is in on the island. I’m gonna take care of their ship first.”  
  
Their ship was docked further into the shallows, they left their board open for anyone to walk into. It took Nelu 9 minutes to see that there was no one on the ship.  
  
“Nothing but a pair of panties, sick bastards...”  
  
Nelu left a fire on their ship near the barrels containing gunpowder. She then goes to a rocky shore to hunt crabs.  
  
After 12 minutes, Nelu had gained 2 crabs of average size and 1 small in size. During that time, there were no signs of the company returning and the fire had fully engulfed their ship. Nelu made a fire on the beach in order to cook the crabs inside a pot of water she grabbed from her ship prior. She tossed in chopped vegetables, the only food she had on the ship, into the pot and added a fair amount of salt and spices she had on her person. After 7 minutes of boiling, the pot started to smell appetizing.  
  
“Even when I don’t have the necessary ingredients, I still have it in me.”  
  
Nelu extinguished the fire and placed the pot on a rock.  
  
“Time to dig in!”  
  
Nelu started with eating the vegetables first, blowing on her spoon to cool the food. After finishing the vegetables, the stew is now colder than before, but still hot enough to cook the crabs. Nelu blew on the stew and drank it 3 gulps leaving the crabs for last.  
  
“Hah… Finally!”  
  
Nelu opens the small crab by first cracking it with the hilt of her sword and prying it with her spoon. Nelu comments how good it is and finishes it in a series of cracking and prying.  
  
Nelu finishes her meal in 17 minutes. She picks up her equipment and sees that her opposition’s ship has finally fallen on itself due to the fire. Nelu climbs up the rope to her ship with an indifferent look on her face. She raised her anchor and set course to another destination.
