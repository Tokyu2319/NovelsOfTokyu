# 11·11·1126  
11:11 — Sea of Franset.  
Cloud coverage: 2 oktas[^1]  
Wind: 4 m/s from 268º[^2]  
To recapitulate, Nelu had successfully stolen a merchant ship and is now exploring its contents.  
  
Nelu seems confident that the ship is empty. After checking the ship for 10 minutes, Nelu confirms that the ship is empty.  
  
Nelu: Hyoo! \<That just leaves the cabin.>  
  
Nelu wiggles the door open. It is locked and requires a key, Nelu does not have the key.  
  
Nelu: <Looks like I’ll have to use a bit of force on this one>  
  
Nelu fires her pistol on the lock, successfully opening the door. Nelu finds difficulty in opening the door as it rubs against the floorboards. Nelu rams the door open. Nelu is able to output the necessary force to open the door. Nelu is out of breath and visibly panting.  
  
Nelu: Hah… <Hellooo! Anyone in heeere?>  
  
There is nobody in the room. The room contains a wardrobe, a wine rack, bookshelves, a bed built by the hull of the ship, a drawer underneath the bed, a chair and a table. Nelu inspects the bed.  
  
Nelu: <Nice! Thought it’d be smaller, very happy with this. I’m not touching it, though. Who knows how many bed bugs there are in it. I’ll clean you up later.>  
  
Nelu checks the drawer to inspect its contents. Nelu finds silver and jewelry, but later found a compartment hidden behind a board.  
  
Nelu: <Oh? What’s this?>  
  
Nelu finds a folded piece of vellum which she initially assumed to be a map, she is correct. Nelu does not recognize where it points to.  
  
Nelu: <That’s definitely not Yurova. I know that.>  
  
Writing on the back of the map shows coordinates and instructions.  
  
Nelu: <A treasure of sorts? Exciting. I’m assuming there’s treasure, but I’ll have to know what this map maps out.>  
  
Nelu seems to drop the idea for now and focuses on settling in the ship. Nelu proceeds with drying the bed first, she takes the bed’s bedsheet off and places it on the railings. Nelu picks up a ramrod, a tool used for loading cannonballs into cannons, and uses it to whack the bed.  
  
Nelu: Ei, ei, ei!  
  
11:34  
Nelu leaves the bed to dry. Nelu picks up a feather duster from the cabin to dust off said room. Nelu started with the bookshelves.  
  
Nelu: <Navigation, manuals, medicine, maritime Laws, yada-yada…>  
  
Nelu finds a book containing instructions for identifying and avoiding sodomites, as well as legal procedures governing the use of torture to extract confessions from them during trials.[^3] Nelu covers her mouth with her left hand while her other hand holds the book. Nelu closes the book and takes it outside to throw it into the sea. Nelu‘s face appears disgusted.  
  
Nelu: <I feel dirty now…>  
  
11:39  
Nelu heads to the galley of the ship.[^4] Nelu arrives at the galley and picks up a brush from a tub and puts it in a bag. Nelu heads to the hold of the ship.[^5] Nelu arrives at the hold and inspects a barrel of full of grog.[^6] Nelu proceeds to inspect more barrels full of grog until deciding to roll one up onto the main deck.  
  
11:49  
Nelu discards the contents of the barrel into the sea. Nelu opens her bag and grabs her soap, knife, and brush. Nelu shaves her soap into the empty barrel and after shaving a small amount of soap, she puts the soap inside her bag. Nelu pours water from her canteen into the barrel and begins cleaning the barrel. After cleaning the barrel for 8 minutes, Nelu pours water inside the barrel to rinse the soap off then discard its contents.  
  
11:57  
After making sure that the barrel is clean, Nelu puts her hand over the barrel and uses hylogenesis[^7] to fill the barrel with water. The temperature of the water inside the barrel shows 17ºC. Nelu used manokinesis[^8] to heat up the water inside the barrel to a desired temperature. The temperature of the water inside the barrel shows 38ºC. Nelu takes off her bag, her garments, her undergarments, and unties her hair. Nelu puts herself feet first into the warm water.  
  
Nelu: Haaa… <That’s nice. Haven’t used magic that much. It’s a lot of mana[^9], but it’s not like I’m gonna use magic all that often out here.>  
  
12:33  
Nelu: Ah. <I forgot the towel. No one’s looking, right? I can— Who am I kidding? I just bathed in the open!>  
  
Nelu lifts herself off the barrel and heads towards the cabin. Inside she finds a towel and is able to dry herself. After drying herself for 3 minutes, Nelu puts her hair in a towel wrap to dry it out. Nelu goes back to the barrel and to wash her clothes. Nelu shaves her soap and begins washing her clothes for 7 minutes  
  
12:43  
Nelu finishes washing her clothes and leaves her them to dry in the sun. Nelu goes back to the cabin again and searches for something.  
  
Nelu: <This is stupid. I’ve found more maps than towels. This is actually stupid. Well, if it makes me feel better I know where to stop for food. This vacant island will do, I’m hungry for some crabs>  
  
Nelu heads to the helm of the ship. This is Nelu’s second time trying to operate a ship after the heist she did in order to escape this ship’s owners and the law enforcers. Despite Nelu’s lack of experience, she quickly learned to steer her ship towards her desired location.

Cloud coverage: 2 oktas  
Wind: 3.8 m/s from 269º

Nelu: \<Looks like company.>

Nelu steps back from the helm as a lead ball was fired from the shore toward her. Nelu’s ship was 92 m from the shore. Nelu’s movement preceded the projectile’s arrival by approximately 0.8 seconds. No apparent auditory, visual, or physical stimulus was identified that would reasonably explain the movement.[^10]  
  
Nelu: <Huh. Maybe they just— misfired?>  
  
Nelu stepped back again and another ball passes her.[^10]  
  
Nelu: <Okay, these folks don’t seem that friendly.>  
  
Nelu draws her pistol and aims it at the shooter. Nelu fires her weapon and the assailant who shot first drops against the sand. The other assailants shot at Nelu while the ones who did not engage in the fight retreated into the foliage. The assailants are unable to hit Nelu while Nelu is steadily walking down from the helm and aiming at the assailants with one hand on her pistol.  
  
Nelu: <Two, three… four, five… six, and seven.>  
  
Nelu fired her weapon an additional 6 times and 6 assailants dropped against the sand. Nelu reloads her pistol with another magazine.  
  
Nelu: <The rest of them probably don’t have guns. I’ll just use my sword to fend them off if they return.>  
  
Nelu anchors her ship near the shore and goes down to the shallows by rope. Nelu proceeds to search the seven bodies. All of which have muskets on their person. One had whiskey which Nelu took for herself. 7 are confirmed deceased and among them was Pierre Dubois.  
  
Nelu: <It’s just me and how many is in that ship and how many is in this island. I’m gonna take care of their ship first.>  
  
Their ship was docked further into the shallows and they had left their board open. Nelu searches the assailants’ ship.

Nelu confirms that there’s no one on the ship. Nelu heads to the hold of the ship and grabs a matchbox from her bag. Nelu lights a match and ignites a fire in the hold. Nelu leaves their ship then heads to a rocky shore to hunt crabs.

Nelu made a campfire on the beach in order to cook the crabs inside a pot of water she grabbed from her ship prior. She tossed in chopped vegetables, the only food she had on the ship, into the pot and added a fair amount of salt and spices she had in her bag.

Nelu picks up her equipment and looks at the assailants’ ship. Their ship has finally fallen on itself due to the fire. Nelu climbs up the rope, raises her ship’s anchor, and set course to another destination.
