# 12.11.1126  
  
05.30 — Port city of Hinland.  
Cloudy. Calm wind.  
  
Nelu wakes up from the bed belonging to the brothel she stayed for the night. She did not have intercourse with the worker. She picks up her belongings and ties her hair again into a bun. She then applies a substance (a combination of donkey milk and arrowroot[^1] starch) below her eyes, covering her Region-Unique Birthmark.  
  
[^1]: Arrowroot is a plant native to Alkeluna. The designation is derived from its morphological similarity to the terrestrial plant of the same name.
