This is really bad.  
  
It runs and it drags my back against the green ground. Pushing further and pounding me against some rocks it found. I found leverage and grabbed one of its arms.  
  
***Crack!***  
  
It did not shriek but it did throw me away. I cannot see with these eyes. The stars do not illuminate my surroundings. I need to keep its focus on me.  
  
“HEY!” I shouted.  
  
Hmm… The situation was better when it held me.  
  
I can feel it running around, but *feeling* isn’t enough. To see better, I rub off the paste under my first eyes. Ren’s doing. “My bad, Ren.”  
  
My second pair adjusted to the darkness, yet, the animal did not reveal itself.  
  
“BAD DOG! COME AT ME!”  
  
I crouched, putting my body low, prepared for another swipe. For as long as I can remember, the animal never made a sound. Even at this very moment, it refuses to be loud.  
I cannot rely on my hearing.  
  
Feel the ground. Smell its scent. Look at a direction.  
  
***Sniff.***  
  
Nothing but dirt…  
  
***Sniff***  
  
I smell Ren.  
  
No. I won’t let you.  
  
Fearing the worse, I run as fast as I can. Running to where Ren is.  
  
Then… I feel a swipe coming in.  
Behind me, to the left.  
Readying my legs I jumped to the left. It had no time to slow down. Midair. I am on top of the animal, clasping my hands.  
This time, I got the drop on it.  
  
”Hiyah!”  
  
I hold tightly to its dark body. The animal is still running around after that. Disoriented. Taking the opportunity, my legs clutch to its body, then I raise my hands and plunge them down on the same black spot I hit.  
  
I break, break, break, hoping to crush its skull.  
  
I feel a grip on both of my legs, then a crushing force.  
  
“AUGH—!”  
  
My thighs cannot hold on anymore. It twists its head to grab me. Now I’m held by its maw, swinging me around hoping to break or disorient me, as I did to it.  
  
This animal is a large black wolf. Arms protrude out of its dark spots. Human arms. That’s what crushed me, ow. Its teeth are made to maim, digging deeper while swinging me like a rag.  
  
Ow. Please stop.  
  
I can’t feel my feet and my head is swelling.  
  
My hands won’t reach its eyes.  
  
The stars are blurring.  
  
The swinging keeps pushing blood to my head.  
  
I’m growing weaker. Ren, you need to run.  
  
Ren… I promised.  
  
*Protect.*  
  
Gah! Its teeth shattered and its gums ruptured. Finally, I’m free.  
It flails around, gushing blood from its mouth. Still not making a sound.  
  
I put my hands on the ground and felt a warmth. The warmth goes through my body, then to my legs. They’re fine. My chest and my belly are fine. Blood covered in their place but I’m fine.  
  
My legs fill with power, like a hundred horses. I can run towards the animal with great speed.  
  
For what reason? It is weak. I can deliver a single punch. A punch with the power of a hundred silverbacks.  
  
My punch makes a squelching impact. Its jaws, chest, and even its front legs are broken apart.  
  
I don’t want to look at it. I run as fast as I could towards the smell where Ren is.  
  
—The remaining hunters are seen with their horses and wagons. One of them notified an approaching presence only to realize it’s Kell returning. Ren was the first to run up to her. Following suit, Kell crouched to her height.  
  
“I didn’t— I never doubted… Don’t go leaving like that!”  
  
Kell managed to catch Ren into a hug. Patting and rubbing her back.  
  
“You— ***Sniffe.*** You look fine?”  
  
“Yeah. Give me your hand.”  
  
“Wha? What’s this?”  
  
“One of its teeth.”  
  
“It’s bigger than my eye! You’re telling me you killed it—?”  
  
Jim butted in. “Girls? May I please have a look?”  
  
Ren handed her the tooth. Its ebony shined in the morning sun.  
  
“Is that true. Did you kill it?” Arthur asked Kell.  
  
“Yem. A? Yes!”  
  
”How!?” In unison.  
  
Kell decided best to summarize it. “It chomped on me but I was stronger. I broke apart its jaw.”

“So, are we gonna haul its body back? To Hinland I mean.” Arthur said.  
  
“Nah, after what we’ve seen? We’re leaving here.”  
  
”Are we coming with?” Ren said.
