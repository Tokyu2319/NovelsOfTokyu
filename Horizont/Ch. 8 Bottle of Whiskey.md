Fathers, sons, and grandfathers.  
Men who are strong and men who are smart.  
Men who lost some and men who lost all.  
  
They follow not because of her words. Not because she’s promised them anything. Not because she shares their hatred.    
They follow her because she is right. Every time. Implausible. Hard to believe. Yet they yield impossible victories.  
  
An old man with glasses walks up from the road. “They’re set, captain.” cold winds billow through the forest.  
  
“Good. Let’s head back.” said the young woman. The snow’s glare seemed unable to pierce her eyes.  
  
A camp hidden near the road. Inside comprised a group of abled men. They are evenly divided into two age groups. Only a couple of them are noticeably younger than the rest. Everyone here holds a sword which they keep in their scabbards.  
Despite the harsh cold they’re able to keep themselves full. Not because they have an abundance of it, but the number of men they need to feed. We can see fifteen people in this group, including their leader, Nelu.  
  
“D’you think there’d be more than three?”  
  
“I hope not! At most, those bombs can only take two.”  
  
“Hah. If you’re so wary, why don’t you ask the captain?”  
  
“I-I don’t think that’s how that works…”  
  
An exchange between four men.  
  
Another voice butted in. “You boys are being scaredy cats. These soldiers came from the capital. They probably haven’t even seen the war!”  
  
“Did you just forget!? We had to let a greenhorn focus on the captain!”  
  
“Ah! You’re right… But they could’ve secretly been an ace y’know?”  
This counts six.  
  
“So what? We’ve to assume all of them are like that?”  
Now seven.  
  
“Correct.”  
  
All of them zipped their mouths shut in response to their leader. Or rather their “captain” as they’ve said. I guess it would make sense, since they’re surrounded by water. Cold, packed, and white water.  
  
Nelu squats down towards the campfire. “Calm yourselves. They’ll arrive in about an hour. Be ready until then, okay boys?”  
  
“Sir yes sir!”  
“Understood captain.”  
“Aye sir.”  
  
A humble group. knitted together under Nelu’s hands. Founded in the heart of Dogel.  
They took their time to adjust themselves. Maintaining the sharpness of their blades. Cleaning the barrels of their guns. Keeping themselves awake, although that’s an easy task considering their feet are deep in snow.  
It’s like they’re preparing for a battle instead of an ambush.  
  
Some time has passed. What they’re looking for can probably be seen from the snowy plains right now.  
  
“Time’s up. Positions everyone.”  
  
Everybody took their positions and, as if right on cue, four trucks are caught in Nelu’s spyglass. They are a ways off in the distance. Driving at a steady pace. Ample time before the group grits their teeth.  
  
Nelu said to the old man beside her. “Four trucks, doctor.”  
  
“Four!?” The doctored whispered. “Do you think we can take that many, captain?”  
  
”***Cough.*** Give me.” Nelu held her palm open. “The grenade. Just one.” Nelu then rasped an instruction to him.  
  
Relaying the raspy information. “Everyone! We’re expecting FOUR trucks!” he gestures to the group.  
  
“Two trucks explode that I get. How do we take care of the last two!”  
  
“Fitz will throw a grenade right after those two. Ah! Nelu’s throwing you one now!”  
  
The young man named Fitz catches the volatile weapon. “Oi! Captain! That almost killed me!”  
  
“Once you take them out! We shoot from here and Nelu will take care of the last truck!”  
The group is divided by the road. The doctor’s shouting is needed to give information.  
  
The trucks enter from the snowy plains into the forest. Where the ambush is located. The leading trucks will get most of the explosion and, after waiting for a few minutes, that’s exactly what happens.  
  
***Kaboom!***  
  
The two trucks ahead are covered in smoke. It is evident that their engines contributed to the detonated explosion. The two trucks behind pushed their brakes and shouting can be heard from inside the vehicles. Before the drivers could get out, their windows were met with Potato mashers. They’re not enough to kill them, however. At most it would disorient them. The soldiers who got hit ejected from their vehicle, their hands on the road’s ground. They were shot on the spot. The last remaining truck geared in reverse.  
Nelu ran towards the last truck while her men followed behind.  
  
Three shots ring out from forest. One man from the bushes shot the tires flat. Now Nelu can catch up it while the men behind her covered for her.  
  
The driver retaliates. The windshield breaks and shots were fired straight at Nelu. No shots hit her. She simply moved out of the way, as if she read the soldier’s moves behind the foggy glass. She pulled out a handgun of her own and quickly dispatched the driver.  
Nelu steadies herself before approaching the last survivor. Headshot. A clear certainty of death. Nelu pulled the cadaver out of the vehicle then chopped off its head with her sword.  
  
”***Cough, cough.*** Make sure they’re dead everyone.”  
Her men follow suit. Pulling out their corpses and beheading them.  
  
Even the recently enlisted soldiers learned how to survive from such a grievous wound. Decapitating them ensured they wouldn’t survive. To ease their minds, Nelu’s men would make *sure* to separate the head from the body.  
A lopped off head is lobbed from the forest.  
  
When her men was busy doing business. Nelu rummages through the trucks supplies.  
Munition, rations, medicine, and luxuries such as socks and coffee. Nelu returns to her men with a bottle of whiskey in hand.  
  
“Dibs on this.” Nelu stated. “Take everything from these trucks. After we’re done we’re walking to the front.”  
  
Nelu’s group successfully raided the trucks and halted their supply lines, but only for a moment. Interrupting logistics is but a short-term solution, they can always use another road. The group walked near the road but never in the open. They’ll often set up camps to skip the night. Always with one pair of eyes open.  
The leader of the group is seen drinking her woes away in the camp. It came to the point where she would collapse in front of her men.  
  
“Nelu, sir! Leave some for tomorrow.” a young adult tended to her. “Why’d we even let you finish the bottle?”  
  
It is what it is. She can’t Rest for the life of her. Not when she’s full of boiling hatred. A fiery temperament that is surrounded by the cold. You’d think two opposite temperatures cancel out, but it only makes things worse for her sleep. Only alcohol can douse that flame of hers.  
  
Her hand let’s the bottle slip from her grip.  
Nelu wakes up to see herself surrounded by the sand. Stars illuminate her way forth. She packs up and continues her journey.
