I woke up from the light shining from the window. The woman in my arms is still asleep. I’m in Hinland. Inside a brothel.  
After selling the jewelry for some silver I went on a shopping spree for some snacks. Last night, Hinland was holding a festival to celebrate their victory in the war. It’s weird. I get stares now and then but they’re not *hostile* stares. It makes sense. No one would want to be accused of being a Supremacist after the war. Yeah. I should probably head back to the ship.  
How’d I get to Hinland? Well, I rowed here. Hid my ship in an inlet, obviously. Anyways, I managed to row back to the ship, the SSD. Short for the Safest Ship on Dunya!  
  
The reason I stayed at the brothel was because I didn’t sleep last night... But because of that! I managed to uncover a lot about the “treasure” map. Alkeluna! It maps out South Alkeluna. A continent that Hilda taught me.  
  
I pinched the bridge of my nose. “How could I forget such a thing?”  
  
The biggest continent in the world, divided into North Alkeluna and South Alkeluna. However, Hilda never taught me of Alkeluna’s countries or politics, only its geography. With that said the biggest challenge here is that I can’t bring the SSD on land.  
The only river that I can fit this ship into is all the way down of North Alkeluna—the rift that divides the two regions, given the name Weltenstrom… They spread like ants I tell you.  
  
The rift is the shortest water passage to the eastern part of the world. Not like I have any business there, but because of that, the rift will be busy. No way around it. Yup. It’s decided. I’m on an adventurer to Alkeluna!  
  
I raised the anchor, unfurled the sails, and took the helm.  
I can always make a few stops on my way. Resupplying and whatnot.  
I’m now in the open ocean. No land in sight, only the horizon around me. According to my navigation. I’m west of Franset and, right now, I’m heading towards the Andalian Peninsula.  
  
—The priest twirls and fidgets with his pen.  
  
Ugh. My mind had wandered for a moment. I gripped my pen tightly and wrote a detailed report of today. So much had gone wrong that—I’m deciding to just end it all. Get over with it. I’ve grown too old to build anything now. I gently placed my pen on the paper then walked out from the church. The building was built upon a cliff overlooking the sea. The steps needed to get here reminds us of our suffering. The view from up here is rather beautiful. The sun sets to the west, where the cliff is facing. From there I saw a ship. I adjusted my glasses to see it unmanned. Weird. It’s going to crash against the cliff’s face. Wait, no. Never mind. There’s a person onboard and they look like they’re taking the stone stairs. I hurried towards them in order to prevent them from knowing about the door.

”Then why would a *wandering sailor* have a merchant ship.”

“I sailed from Franset.”  
  
“Then I don’t care what you do here. You can leave now.”  
  
“Sanctuary!” she shouted. “I seek sanctuary!”  
  
Is she serious?  
  
“Come with.”  
  
“Wait it’s that easy?”

“Resupplying. Running low on food on the ship.”  
  
“We have fish and vegetables in storage. It should be enough to feed one person. I’m assuming there’s only one person there, yes?”  
  
“Yes.”

“Ah geez. Surely you jest~” I regret flattering her. Him. Her. Why’s she dressed like a boy? With her hair tied up like that.  
  
I guided her towards the church’s doors and opened them.  
  
“Take what you need and leave.”  
  
“What you don’t like my company?” she said in a sassy tone.  
  
She didn’t come off as unwelcoming. Nothing exudes an oceanic smell from here. She smelled like a clean babe after a bath. Maybe the stress was getting to me. I adjusted my tone to her. She came back from the storeroom with bags of food in both hands.

“Yes I have.”

“I became a priest right after.”

“It’s complicated.”

“That I don’t know.”
