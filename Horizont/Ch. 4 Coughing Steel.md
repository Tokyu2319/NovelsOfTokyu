”Kell!” she shouted “Kell!!”  
  
Ren was meandering around in one of the trenches. The sun was above them which meant that the shadows were almost unobservable.  
  
“Coming!” Kell yelled.  
  
“I need help getting up. This world isn’t meant for small people.”  
  
Kell lifted Ren atop the edge of the trench.  
  
“What did you find down there?”  
  
“Just more dirt.” Ren answered. “What are you wearing?”  
  
“Oh, This? I found it on the ground.”  
  
“Throw it away. It’s dirty. Wouldn’t wanna mess up the braid I gave you.”  
  
“Okay…” Kell said as she tossed her hat.  
  
It is at this time where the siblings are fully clothed, albeit primitive in fashion. Draped in various animal hide and makeshift thread.  
  
“I think I see a blockhouse over there.” Ren said. “Let’s check it out.”  
  
“I don’t get it. Why are we walking there?”  
  
“Enjoy the process and whatnot.”  
  
“I thought you enjoy me carrying you?”  
  
“I did get used to it but sometimes I’d like to take my time. Walking.”  
  
“Anything in there?” Kell asked.  
  
“There’s copper here. You reckon if people’ll find these valuable?”  
  
“Can I see?”  
  
“Here.” she handed her the munitions.  
  
“Ah! Ah! Don’t crush it!”  
  
“There’s more than this, yes? One crushed copper won’t amount much.”  
  
“I guess…”  
  
“There’s blackpowder inside of them.”  
  
Ren felt an alert crawl up her spine. “Stay still.”  
  
“Why?”  
  
“I’m pretty sure that’s dangerous but… I don’t know. Just be careful with it.”  
  
“What should I do? I mean, I know you just told me to stay still”  
  
”Spit on it?”  
  
“Okay.”  
  
***Pfft.***  
  
“On the powder not the copper.”  
  
“Oh.”  
  
***Pfft.***  
  
“What now?”  
  
“I think we’re safe to stay away now. Let’s get out of here.”  
  
They’ve separated themselves from the concrete house. Crossing over the many trenches and craters.  
  
“What were you expecting to find here, Ren?”  
  
“Something to sell or something. I was hoping to find bodies here.”  
  
“But we haven’t met any people offering to sell, no? In fact, I haven’t met anyone else except Papa and you.”  
  
“Yeah, yeah. Figuring that out comes later. About anyone will accept anything.”  
  
“If you say so.”  
  
The siblings went on a walk heading towards a random direction. Occasionally, Kell would hunt for food and both of them would rest. At times they were committed to trekking these lands, Kell would let Ren ride on her back to preserve both of their energy.  
  
”Heads up.” Ren clicked her fingers in a direction.  
  
“I know this one!”  
  
”You know? This one?”  
  
”It’s a hunk of steel!” she smiled with glee.  
  
”Eh. You’re not wrong. Take us closer to that thing.”  
  
Kell carried towards the large unknown instrument.  
  
”There’s a tube sticking out.” Kell pointed out.  
  
Ren held Kell‘s shoulders furiously. “Don’t look at hole.”  
  
“Is something gonna bite me?”  
  
“Gut feeling. Just trust me.”  
  
“Right. Hey. There’s a— uh. A latch?”  
  
”The opening! Can you put me near it?”  
  
Kell sat Ren down on the steel chassis. Ren opened the latch and went inside without warning.  
  
“Ren!? Are you okay in there?”  
  
“It’s surprisingly spacious here. Looks like I found a place where I belong!” the voice from the instrument muffled.  
  
“Do you think I’d fit in there?”  
  
“No! You don’t!” Ren’s shouting bounces off the metal box.  
  
—Click, click, click, click, click…  
  
“What are you doing in there?”  
  
“I’m figuring it out! Somewhat?”  
  
“What do you mean?”  
  
“I think this thing can move!”  
  
“Can you repeat that?”  
  
The steel instrument coughed and coughed until it roared itself awake. Dark smoke exited from its pipes. Then a whirling of blades can be heard faintly.  
  
“Oh! The-the the uh. Head! The head and is moving! Are you doing this, Ren?”  
  
“What! What am I doing! Say that again!”  
  
The barrel protruding from the head stopped for a moment. Kell took the opportunity to jump on its top and stick her head in the latch Ren climbed in.  
  
“What are you doing!?” she said with her head upside down.  
  
“You tell me! What did it look like?”  
  
“It was moving but then it stopped. Are you controlling it?”  
  
“That might be it. Let’s see.” Ren looked at one of the pulleys.  
  
Kell took her head out to see the wheelless wagon moving. She stuck her head inside again.  
  
Making a smile. “We’re moving, Ren.”  
  
“I see that.”  
  
“You can see in here?”  
  
“I’m looking in this pipe, see? Oh right, you can’t fit in here.”  
  
The wagon continued forward. Unimpeded.  
  
“Hey, Ren.”  
  
“Yeah?”  
  
“Can you spin the head round and around?”  
  
“Alright.”  
  
The wagon stopped then spun it’s head clockwise.  
  
“Huh. It’s not as fast as I thought. Continue moving please. I’m tired of walking.”  
  
“Alright. Looks like we’re taking this for now.”  
  
The siblings continued in their aimless journey. Eventually, they had to leave their wagon behind. Perhaps it was on its last leg. Kell carried her sleeping sister on her back~.
