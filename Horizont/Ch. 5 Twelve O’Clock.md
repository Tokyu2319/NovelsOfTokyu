”Op, op, op! Rise and shine little one!”  
The man with a hat loosened his grip on the gun. The crackling of fire can be heard. It’s light keeping away the surrounding darkness.  
  
Ren sat upwards from Kell’s lap, befuddled. “Kell. What’s going on? Who are these people?”  
  
“We— I mean, I found a settlement while you were sleeping. Said they came from Hinland.” Kell said.  
  
“You didn’t bother to wake me up!?”  
  
“Uhh… I didn’t want to. Plus, they weren’t bad.”  
  
“Fair enough. So, who are these people?”  
  
“You can introduce yourselves now.”  
  
“Nice to meet you Ren. I’m Jim and that there’s my son, Arthur.”  
“Arthur here!”  
 In the distance, the young one with a hat can be seen tending to his weapon.  
  
Ren took a moment to think. “Am I getting that right? Jim and Arthur?”  
  
“Mhm. You don’t speak Anglo, Ren?” Kell said.  
  
”No, wait. Hold on. I’m figuring it out.” Ren said in her native tongue.

“Ren’s a bit rusty, right now.”

“Ha! She can speak it!” Arthur said, walking towards their circle.  
  
Jim offered Ren something hard and dry to her. “Here. Your dinner for tonight.”  
  
Hesitantly, she took it. “Nn. Thanks.”  
  
Arthur stood up from his seat. “I’ll leave you two be. Arthur.”  
“Yes sir?”  
“Tone down the racism, yeah?”  
  
“I wasn’t— That wasn’t my intention!” Arthur pulled his hat down, obscuring his eyes. “I’ll… Also be leaving you two alone.”  
  
“What was that?” Ren said to Kell.  
  
“I don’t know. He said something about me coming from Alkeluna.”  
“Alkeluna?”  
“Yeah, he thought people from the east came from—”  
  
Cutting Kell off. “Alkeluna, where is that?”  
  
“Right here.” Kell pointed down.  
  
“Wait, hold on— You said Hinland before, right?”  
  
“Yyyes. They said it’s north from here. Where we came from.”  
  
“What!?”  
  
“What’s wrong?”  
  
Tapping her nails on her forehead. “How far down did we walk?”  
  
“Not that far I think?”  
  
“Okay that’s reassuring. We just need to—”  
  
“I forgot to mention we’re helping them hunt.”  
  
“What!? Help with what now?”  
  
“The Hundred Dogs.”  
  
“The Hundred— Why?”  
  
“To do good.”  
  
Remembering her Promise. “I mean— Okay. Are we getting paid to do this?”  
  
“They don’t promise it.”  
  
“Then—!”  
  
“It’s fine, Ren. I’ll make them feel… What’s the word? Indebted!”  
  
“HOW?”  
  
“It’s just a bunch of animals. I’ve hunted plenty before.” Kell smugly said.  
  
Ren massaged her temples.  
The siblings continued to banter the fire away until Arthur told them to sleep in one of their wagons. Finding much comfort under a roof, both of them slept there for the night.  
  
Today is the day they hunt The Hundred Dogs. This wasn’t the first time the hunters searched for their target. They have naught a clue on its whereabouts. Only witnesses of destruction and proof of razed villages, whose terror belongs to that of many wolves. Fifteen men, including Jim and his son, took the offer to hunt down this so-called atrocity.  
Three canines lead the front, sniffing for clues of a hundred dogs. Following these canines are their caretakers, behind them is their leader, with Kell, Ren, and Arthur by his side.  
  
Ren was eyeing at Jim’s pocket. Noticing that, Jim confronted her. ”Whatcha looking at, hmm?“  
  
“Ahah, nothing…” she nervously said.  
  
Jim tossed her the handgun. “Here.”  
  
“Huh? Oof! That’s heavy.”  
  
“It’s not loaded.”  
  
“What is it? It feels nice.”  
  
“A handgun from Dogel.”  
  
“This looks really different. Different from one of your guns.”  
  
“Dogel’s tech. It rivals many countries.”  
  
Inspecting the spectacle, she inquired Jim about its many features. Including its safety, magazine, barrel, rate of fire, and the bullets it used which Jim had ran out of.  
Just then, the sun was about to set.  
  
“Everyone! We’ll call it off today. Let’s head back.” Jim ordered his men.  
  
Another unsuccessful search for these men. Ren was getting anxious if this would go on for days, luckily for her, she wouldn’t stay here for long. That’s because they we’re only eight. Compared to fifteen. Half their men are missing. Seven men were lost.  
  
“Everyone! With me! Circle formation!” Jim shouted at the top of his lungs.  
  
“The hell’s going on...”  
“Where’d the fuck they go?”  
“Bill? Bill, where are you!”  
  
The canines are disturbed, they finally caught something. They barked at whichever the danger was coming from.  
  
“Stay calm everyone! Keep those dogs quiet!”  
  
Shoulder to shoulder, everyone is silenced in their circle. Ren was able to climb up Kell‘s back.  
  
“Ren, do you see anything?” Kell said.  
  
”Nothing. I can’t see anything.” she whispered, holding on tightly. “We need to run. Now.” quietly she said.  
  
Gritting her teeth. “I can’t do that.”  
  
“You don’t know what you’re facing. This isn’t worth it.”  
  
“I left seven of them missing. I can’t do that again.”  
  
Ren took a moment of silence. Everyone stayed like this for a while. Quiet. Looking for the slightest movement.  
  
“I have a plan.” Ren said aloud.  
  
“What?”  
“Shh!”  
”Hush!”  
  
“What is it?” Tom offered an ear.  
  
“Does it involve us running, Ren?” Kell said disappointed from before.  
  
“That’s not— Can you take it, Kell?”  
  
“Take what?”  
  
“You. Move out from the circle.”  
  
Kell placed Ren from her back to the ground. “You want me to let this thing take me?”  
  
“That’s the idea.”  
  
“I like that idea. It gives the others an opportunity.”  
  
“Wait that’s now what I—” Kell shut her mouth. Without saying anything, she assured her she would return safely.  
  
The others were too focused on keeping their ears open, but they had entrusted the both of them to deal to this ordeal.  
  
“No. T-that can’t be right.” Arthur said.  
  
“You confident in this Kell?”  
“Pops! No…”  
  
“Yes I am.”  
  
“Go ahead. We’ll cover you.”  
  
“I’ll be leaving you with them, Ren.”  
  
The remaining hunters moved out of their way, letting Kell break from the formation. She walked further and further. Jim and Ren kept watch while the others covered every other angle.  
Then nothing. Had they been hallucinating all the time?  
No. Seven men were missing during that time. It’s unsure whether they strayed from the group. Possibilities like that are hard to ponder when you’re surrounded by darkness.  
  
“A-are we safe?” said one of the hunters.  
“Don’t jinx it!”  
“Man, shut up!”  
  
Deep in thought. ”…If Kell makes herself big then maybe, just maybe, doing that will make it show up. It’s stupid but that’s all I got.” she whispered to herself. “Kell! Make some noise!” Ren announced.  
  
“What?” they all collectively said.  
  
“What’s that gonna do?” Arthur asked.  
“Yeah.”  
“We don’t even know if it’s a pack!”  
  
“Just do it!” Ren said.  
  
“Kell! You heard her.” Jim relayed to her.  
  
Taking the suggestion, Kell readied herself. By then, Jim saw something darker than the trees. From his peripheral, it approached from the left.  
  
“TWELVE O’CLOCK!”  
  
Everyone looks to where Jim is watching. What they saw was a streak of darkness with no sound to follow it. They did not see Kell standing there a moment ago. They pointed their guns towards an empty field. No shots were fired.  
  
“F-fall back!”  
  
All of them ran back, heading to the settlement. Ren had difficulty catching up.  
  
“Wha-what about Kell?”  
  
Jim tossed his gun at Arthur, he then picked up Ren in his arms.  
  
“Our lives’re what matters now.”
